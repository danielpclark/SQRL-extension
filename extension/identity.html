    Re-key identity

    Re-keying generates a brand-new master key. Your old identity is kept as a "previous identity" so that sites automatically update to the new key the next time you log in. You need your current rescue code; a new rescue code will be generated.

    Your rescue code

    Write this code down and keep it somewhere safe. It is the only way to recover your identity if you forget your password, and it is needed to re-enable or remove accounts and to re-key. It cannot be shown again.
