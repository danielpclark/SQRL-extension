Same-device login (CPS)

Websites detect a local SQRL client by probing http://localhost:25519/. This extension answers that probe and handles the resulting navigation itself, so sites log you straight in without a round trip through their server-side poll. If a site stops detecting the client, re-install the rules.

About

SQRL (Secure Quick Reliable Login) was designed by Steve Gibson of GRC. This extension implements the SQRL client protocol, identity storage (S4), identity lock and textual identities with a Rust core compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing ever leaves your browser except signed, per-site, pseudonymous public keys.
